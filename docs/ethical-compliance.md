# Ethical Guidelines & Compliance

## Principle 1: Autonomy

- The AI must not force the operator to act. It must _suggest_.
- **Requirement:** UI must allow operator to ignore AI flags at any time without penalty.

## Principle 2: Transparency

- Operators must know if the summary is AI-generated or Fallback (Feature 1.9).
- **Requirement:** Add a label "Generated by AI (Confidence: 0.9)" vs "Extracted Fallback".

## Principle 3: Beneficence

- The system should not cause harm.
- **Requirement:** Ensure the "Safe Exit" feature works reliably even if the device is locked or battery is low.
